## SOAP presentation study notes
These generic sample notes illustrate the app and are not a clinical reference.
SOAP stands for Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan.
Subjective contains the reported chief complaint, history, medications, and allergies.
Objective contains measured vitals and observed physical exam findings.
Assessment records the clinician's explicitly stated impression, including uncertainty.
Plan records what the clinician explicitly stated would happen next.
A missing fact should be marked Not stated, not filled from general knowledge.
Document: medication list as reported.
Document: allergies as reported.
