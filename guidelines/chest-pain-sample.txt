Chest pain documentation example checklist
This fictional checklist demonstrates citation and documentation features. It must not guide clinical care.
Document: chief complaint in the patient's own words, including chest pain if reported.
Document: symptom onset and duration.
Document: factors that worsen or relieve chest pain.
Document: associated symptoms that were asked about, with explicit negatives only if stated.
Document: medication list as reported.
Document: allergies as reported.
Document: blood pressure with its units, if measured.
Document: heart rate with its units, if measured.
Document: physical exam findings that were actually observed.
Document: assessment as stated by the supervising clinician; preserve uncertainty.
Document: plan as stated by the supervising clinician.
