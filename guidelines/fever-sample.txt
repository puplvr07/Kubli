Fever / infection documentation example checklist
This fictional checklist is a demonstration source, not a validated guideline.
Document: duration of fever and how temperature was measured.
Document: temperature in the stated unit, if measured.
Document: reported associated symptoms and exposures.
Document: medications already taken, using the exact stated doses.
Document: allergies as reported.
Document: relevant past history as reported.
Document: physical exam findings actually observed.
Document: assessment as stated, without inferring an infection or diagnosis.
Document: plan as stated by the supervising clinician.
